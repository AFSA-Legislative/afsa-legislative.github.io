The record

Documented changes for enlisted members, veterans, retirees, survivors, and families since 1992, and the bills still moving. Each entry shows the rule before, the rule after, AFSA’s part, and where to verify it.

Struck text is the rule before.Underlined text is the rule after.‸ marks something added.

Enacted

2024

Junior enlisted pay, FY25 NDAA

‸ Added: A 14.5% total targeted raise for junior enlisted

The largest in decades. AFSA’s part: listed in AFSA’s documented legislative record.

Public Law 118-159, sec. 601 (signed 2024)

2022

Honoring Our PACT Act

Before: The burden of proof fell on the veteran After: Presumptive conditions for burn pit and airborne-hazard exposure

5.9 million toxic-exposure screenings and 1.2 million+ claims approved, 2022–2025. AFSA’s part: AFSA and The Military Coalition advocated at every step.

Public Law 117-168 (signed 2022)

Still moving

Proposed changes AFSA carried to Capitol Hill at its March 2026 Hill Day. Status figures are as of that month.

Proposed

Major Richard Star Act, for combat-injured retirees with fewer than 20 years

Current: Retirement pay reduced dollar for dollar by VA disability compensation Proposed: Both payments received in full

Affects 52,000+ veterans. 315 House and 77 Senate cosponsors.

H.R. 2102S. 1032

Proposed

Military Family GI Bill Promise Act

Current: Benefits transfer only while serving Proposed: Transfer during service or after separation

Includes dependents added to the family after retirement.

H.R. 4540

Proposed

Pay Our Troops Act (House) and Pay Our Military Act (Senate)

Current: Pay at risk during a lapse in funding Proposed: Pay and allowances continue until funding passes

A backstop to AFSA’s first priority: full-year defense appropriations on time.

S. 3002H.R. 5401

Proposed

IVF for Military Families Act

‸ Proposed: TRICARE covers IVF treatment

Service-connected injuries and deployments can affect a member’s ability to start a family.
