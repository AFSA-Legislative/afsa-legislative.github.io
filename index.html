Changes like these start when the people a rule affects explain it to the offices that can change it. You don’t need a title or a trip to Washington. You need a relationship with the district office that represents you, and Legislative Awareness Week is the place to start one.

Be part of Legislative Awareness Week

One week, every chapter, one message: the quality of life of the enlisted force and their families.

LAW 2026 -- October 18 - 24

  1. 1.

    Find

    Identify the Representative and both Senators who cover your chapter, your installation, and the surrounding communities.

  2. 2.

    Reach out

    Call or email the district office to request a meeting, propose a fireside chat, or invite the Member to a chapter event.

  3. 3.

    Share

    Walk the office through this year’s top priorities and the bills that matter most to enlisted families.

You take part as a private citizen and AFSA member: out of uniform, on personal time, from your personal email.
